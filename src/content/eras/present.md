This is the real starting point, and everything later is built on it. The population is small and **genetically bottlenecked**, since all of it comes from roughly four animals. That makes it vulnerable, but it also makes it a textbook case of the *founder effect*. Rare traits in the founders can become common quickly.

Living alongside the hippos are the species that will shape their future: the **spectacled caiman**, the migratory fish **bocachico**, the **cattle tyrant** that hunts insects from the backs of capybaras, and the **Antillean manatee**, the hippos' closest competitor for submerged plants.
