Ten thousand years is too short for big changes in a large mammal with a generation time of about ten years. It is enough time for **microevolution**, though.

- Hippos that avoid people survive, so the population becomes more **nocturnal and shy**.
- With no dry season to cope with, **breeding becomes year-round**, and herds spread across more of the landscape.
- The first native predators learn to take **calves**.
