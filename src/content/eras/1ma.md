A million years is the time scale on which mammal species usually form. Glacial cycles split the population again and again. Some groups are trapped in river systems during the dry periods, while others are forced onto dry land.

At the same time, **co-evolution** starts in earnest. Fish adapt to water low in oxygen and rich in dung. Birds specialise on the hippos' parasites. Caimans grow larger.
