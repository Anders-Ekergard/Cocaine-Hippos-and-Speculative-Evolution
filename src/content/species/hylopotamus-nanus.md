## Basis in real science
Dwarf hippos have evolved several times: the living pygmy hippo (*Choeropsis liberiensis*) in the rainforests of West Africa, and the extinct dwarf hippos of Cyprus, Crete, Sicily and Madagascar. Forest and islands favour small size in large herbivores.

## Speculation
Groups isolated along forest streams during wet periods become smaller, more solitary and adapted to a varied diet. With no gomphotheres left in South America, a large seed disperser is missing, and the dwarf hippo fills the gap.
