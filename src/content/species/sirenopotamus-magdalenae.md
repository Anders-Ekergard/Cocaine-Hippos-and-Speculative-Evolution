## Basis in real science
Hippos are the closest living relatives of whales and already have dense bones, so they walk along the bottom instead of swimming. Research on aquatic adaptation in hippos and cetaceans suggests that the step towards a more aquatic life has been taken in steps, and more than once.

## Speculation
Competition with the hippos weakens the Antillean manatee in the rivers. Hippos that stay in the water and eat aquatic plants avoid both people and the savanna strider. The body becomes longer, the legs shorter, and the nostrils move further up. It still gives birth in shallow water, which is its weak point.
