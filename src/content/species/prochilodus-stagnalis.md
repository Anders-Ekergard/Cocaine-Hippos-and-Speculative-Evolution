## Basis in real science
Shurin et al. (2020) measured stronger daily oxygen swings in lakes with hippos. Several South American fish groups, including armoured catfish and arapaima, have independently evolved the ability to breathe air.

## Speculation
Bocachicos that stay put in hippo pools all year get access to an endless food supply. The price is the oxygen dips at dawn. Selection favours a vascularised gut or swim bladder for air breathing, and the migratory urge disappears.
