## Basis in real science
Hippos lose water through their skin quickly and are poorly suited to dry land, so this is the most speculative step in the era. But the extinct Asian and European hippos show that the family can vary a lot in build. Today's hippos already graze on land every night.

## Speculation
In dry glacial periods some populations are trapped on the savannas. Those with longer legs and better skin protection walk further between water holes. After a million years they are a separate genus that wallows in mud only a few hours a day.
