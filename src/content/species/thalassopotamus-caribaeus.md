## Basis in real science
Mammals have moved into the sea at least six times: whales, sirenians, pinnipeds, desmostylians, marine sloths and sea otters. The marine sloth *Thalassocnus* in Peru made the transition in a few million years.

## Speculation
River sirens that follow the Magdalena out to the delta find seagrass meadows with no competitors. Over fifteen million years they become fully marine. The hind legs are now only vestiges, and the young are born in the water.
