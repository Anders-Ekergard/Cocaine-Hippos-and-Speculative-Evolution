Today the spectacled caiman is far too small to threaten an adult hippo. Hippo calves weigh only 25–50 kg at birth, though, and that is within the reach of a large caiman.

In South America's past there were caimans of truly enormous size. *Purussaurus* in the Miocene probably weighed several tonnes. The lineage has done it before.
