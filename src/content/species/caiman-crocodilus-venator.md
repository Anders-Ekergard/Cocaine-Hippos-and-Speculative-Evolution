## Basis in real science
Body size in crocodilians responds quickly to food supply. Nile crocodiles in Africa take hippo calves regularly, even though adult hippos are dangerous to them.

## Speculation
Large caiman males that dare to attack calves get more food and more offspring. Over ten thousand years there is clear selection for size along the hippo rivers.
