## Basis in real science
Large mammals hunted by people often become smaller and more nocturnal within a few thousand years, sometimes faster. The founder effect from four animals means low genetic variation, but also that the population can move quickly in new directions.

## Speculation
Without dry seasons, the hippos' annual rhythm is lost. The animals become somewhat smaller, because size is an advantage against lions and against drought, and the Magdalena has neither. Herds are smaller and spread more widely.
