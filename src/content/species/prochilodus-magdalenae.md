shape: fish
summary: A migratory fish that eats mud and detritus. It is one of the most important food fish of the Magdalena.
niche: Detritivore that cleans the bottoms of lakes and rivers
diet: Organic sediment, algae, detritus
size: Up to about 50 cm
habitat: Ciénagas and the main river during migration
confidence: established
---

The bocachico lives on what settles on the bottom. A river full of hippo dung is in theory a feast. The same dung also uses up oxygen, especially in still water during the hot part of the day.
