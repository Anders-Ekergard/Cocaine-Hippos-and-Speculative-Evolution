## Basis in real science
African oxpeckers eat ticks but also open wounds to drink blood. They live in a relationship with large mammals that falls somewhere between mutualism and parasitism. Their alarm calls warn the host.

## Speculation
The cattle tyrant moves on from capybaras to the much larger and more stable hippo backs. It gets a shorter tail and stronger feet for gripping the slippery skin.
