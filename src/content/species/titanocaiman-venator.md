## Basis in real science
*Purussaurus brasiliensis* lived in South America in the Miocene and may have reached 10 m. Giant crocodilians appear repeatedly where there are large prey in the water.

## Speculation
With plenty of large prey in the rivers, the calf-hunting caiman's lineage keeps growing. The jaws become broader and the teeth more conical for gripping thick skin.
