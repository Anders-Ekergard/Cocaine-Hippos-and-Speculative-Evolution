## Basis in real science
Hippo skin is adapted to warmth and water, and moving into cold highlands would be a major change. But mammals have repeatedly evolved fur and fat layers for cold climates, as the woolly rhinoceros and woolly mammoth did.

## Speculation
The forest dwarf hippo follows the streams up the mountains as the Andes keep rising. Peat bogs replace rivers as daytime refuges. The skin gets sparse but thick hair, and the herds use the mud as insulation.
